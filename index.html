About Timeless Compass Explorer
Curated by the Sri Sri Institute for Advanced Research (SSIAR), the research wing of the Art of Living, Timeless Compass Explorer is an interactive evidence explorer for published research on Ancient Systems of Knowledge (ASK). It helps users navigate curated studies across practices, populations, topics, partnerships, years, and geographies, connecting timeless wisdom with modern scientific inquiry.
Help
Use the filters to narrow the database, search by title, abstract, author, or keyword, click charts to filter results, and select any publication to view study-level details.
